release build reviewed for LCDSirPlus 0.3.0. Cargo registry checksums below
are the checksums recorded in Cargo.lock. A checksum identifies a registry
package; it is not a license-file checksum. This inventory is informational
and is not legal advice.

Rust standard library provenance observed for the reviewed build
---------------------------------------------------------------

The reviewed build environment reported rustc 1.97.1
(commit 8bab26f4f68e0e26f0bb7960be334d5b520ea452, 2026-07-14), host and
target x86_64-pc-windows-msvc. Rust standard-library code may be statically
linked into LCDSirPlus.exe and is not represented by a Cargo.lock package
checksum. The installed toolchain's COPYRIGHT-library.html identifies The
Rust Project Developers, states that the standard library is offered under
Apache-2.0 OR MIT except where otherwise noted, and identifies its Unicode
data under Unicode-3.0. The MIT option is elected here, and the Unicode-3.0
terms are reproduced below.
